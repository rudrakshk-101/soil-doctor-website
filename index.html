How long does a soil test take?

45–60 minutes, right on your field. The Soil Doctor device reads 18 parameters and the results are geo-tagged and saved securely.

When should I test my soil?

Ideally about 60 days before sowing. That gives you time to correct deficiencies before the crop goes in. Seasonal testing lets you track change year on year.

Which crops and languages do you support?

Rice, wheat, maize, cotton, soybean, pigeon pea, groundnut, sugarcane, vegetables such as cabbage, potato and tomato, and fruit crops. Advice is available in Hindi, Marathi, Telugu, Kannada, Tamil and English.

Can someone come to my farm?

Yes. Home visits are ₹500 plus travel. For 50 or more tests, write to partnerships@soildoctor.in for bulk pricing.

Can I become a Soil Didi and earn income?

Yes. We train rural women as Soil Didis. You receive a Soil Doctor kit, training and marketing support. Soil Didis earn ₹15,000–30,000 a month from soil testing and advisory, keep 80% of each test fee, and there is no lock-in.

Can I rent the device instead of buying it?

Yes — rental starts at ₹500 a month. The device comes with a 1-year warranty with free repairs in the first year.

How accurate is on-field testing?

Device results are validated against NABL-accredited laboratory results for parity.

Is your CSR program Schedule VII eligible?

Yes. It is eligible under Schedule VII of the Companies Act, 2013. Data is collected with consent, reported with encryption and audit trails, and is compliant with India's DPDP Act, 2023.
